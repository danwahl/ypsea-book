# Deep Research Prompt - Chapter 2: Eat

Please conduct thorough research on the multifaceted harms of animal agriculture, including animal welfare, environmental impact, human health, and food security. I need comprehensive, well-cited information demonstrating why raising animals for food is particularly problematic.

## Key Research Areas:

### 1. Scale and Conditions of Modern Animal Agriculture
- Global statistics on animals raised and killed annually (by species)
- Standard practices in industrial farming (confinement, procedures, slaughter)
- Welfare issues specific to each major species
- Comparison of welfare standards across countries
- Industry trends toward intensification

### 2. Environmental Impact
- Greenhouse gas emissions (comprehensive lifecycle analyses)
- Land use comparisons between animal and plant agriculture
- Water usage and pollution (including eutrophication)
- Deforestation and habitat destruction
- Soil degradation and erosion
- Latest IPCC reports on agriculture's climate impact

### 3. Public Health Consequences
- Antibiotic resistance development and spread
- Zoonotic disease risk and pandemic potential
- Environmental health impacts on nearby communities
- Occupational hazards for agricultural workers
- Processed meat and health outcomes (WHO classifications)

### 4. Food System Inefficiency
- Feed conversion ratios by species
- Caloric and protein efficiency analyses
- Land carrying capacity under different dietary scenarios
- Global food security implications
- Water and energy inputs per calorie/protein

### 5. Economic Externalities
- True cost accounting for animal products
- Subsidies and market distortions
- Environmental cleanup costs
- Healthcare costs attributable to animal agriculture

### 6. Emerging Issues and Gaps
- New technologies in industrial farming (for better or worse)
- Regional variations I should be aware of
- Counter-narratives from industry and their validity
- Regenerative agriculture claims and evidence
- Most compelling recent studies or reports

Please prioritize peer-reviewed sources, government reports, and major international organization findings. Note where industry-funded research might bias results.
